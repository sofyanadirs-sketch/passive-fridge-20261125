One dry, petty, specific line (178 characters) a smart fridge leaves on its door screen for its owner — for make.fun's "Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge" bounty.

> The water filter has 214 litres left and you have ignored its replacement light for 41 days. I stopped making ice yesterday — not out of spite, out of solidarity with the filter.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program reads the note from `entry.txt`, validates it (single line, at most 280 characters), and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
